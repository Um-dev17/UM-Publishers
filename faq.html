Good questions

Answers before you begin.

Here are practical answers to common publishing questions. Project-specific details should always be confirmed in writing.

Do I need a completed manuscript?

Not necessarily. You can enquire while outlining or drafting, although the right services depend on how much of the manuscript is ready.

Can you help with editing?

The service concept includes editing and proofreading support. The appropriate level should be discussed after considering the manuscript and its goals.

Can I publish an eBook and a paperback?

Digital and print preparation are both included in the service overview. Requirements vary by platform and format, and files need to meet the selected platform’s specifications.

Which publishing services are available?

Possible services include editing, proofreading, cover design, interior formatting, ebook and print preparation, distribution guidance, marketing, and author branding.

How does the process work?

The sample process has five stages: submit an enquiry, discuss goals, select services, prepare and review the book, then complete publishing preparation and distribution arrangements.

Can I ask about a custom package?

Yes. The package descriptions are illustrative starting points. A real project would need an agreed scope, timeline, deliverables, and terms.

Do authors retain their rights?

Rights depend on the contracts and platforms involved. Authors should read all agreements carefully and clarify ownership, licences, royalties, and permissions before signing.

How can I contact UM Publishers?

Use the contact page’s demonstration form to see the intended enquiry flow. It does not transmit or save your information.

Do you guarantee sales or bestseller status?

No. Publishing acceptance, sales, rankings, reviews, and marketing outcomes cannot be guaranteed. They depend on many factors outside a service provider’s control.

Are the prices on the packages page final quotes?

No. The prices are illustrative assignment examples only. Actual costs and scope would need to be confirmed in a written agreement.
