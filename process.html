Five clear steps

A collaborative process, from first enquiry to publication preparation.

Our sample workflow is designed to make decisions visible and keep authors involved at each stage.

1

Submit your book enquiry

Share the working title, genre, manuscript stage, and the kind of help you’re looking for.

2

Discuss your manuscript and goals

Talk through the intended audience, format, timeline, priorities, and questions that need answering.

3

Select suitable services

Identify the work that makes sense for the manuscript and agree on scope, deliverables, and expectations.

4

Prepare and review the book

Coordinate the agreed editorial, design, formatting, and review stages, with clear opportunities for author feedback.

5

Complete publishing preparation

Review final files and discuss distribution arrangements and next steps. Actual platform approval and market performance remain outside anyone’s control.

Every good project starts with a conversation.
