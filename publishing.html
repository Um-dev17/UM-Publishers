From draft to ready

A publishing journey you can understand.

Publishing is a series of creative and practical decisions. Here’s a clear overview of the stages authors commonly work through.

1

Manuscript review & preparation

Discuss your genre, audience, manuscript status, and goals. Identify any structural or preparation work before moving forward.

2

Editing & proofreading

Choose the level of editorial support appropriate to the manuscript. Proofreading is usually most useful after larger edits are complete.

3

Cover design & interior formatting

Develop a visual direction and prepare the inside pages for the intended format, with attention to readability and technical requirements.

4

Digital & print preparation

Prepare files for the selected formats, such as an ebook, paperback, or hardcover, and review specifications before submission.

5

Distribution choices & launch planning

Compare suitable platforms, metadata, pricing considerations, and promotional next steps. Platform acceptance and commercial results are never guaranteed.

Author rights

Your work matters.

Authors should read all service and publishing agreements carefully. Rights, licensing, royalties, fees, and responsibilities depend on the specific agreement and platform selected.

Format options

One story, different reading experiences.

Depending on the project, you may consider an ebook, paperback, hardcover, or a combination. Each format has its own design and file requirements.

  • Digital reading formats
  • Print-on-demand or other print arrangements
  • Metadata and distribution setup
  • Launch planning to support discoverability
